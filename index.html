A structured map of people, networks, public coalitions, denominational actions, and institutional trajectories in and around the Presbyterian Church in America. Every displayed connection is designed to resolve back to a source.

What the index means

The Network Involvement Index measures recurrence and organizational involvement across the included datasets. It is not a theology score and does not imply agreement with every position taken by an organization or co-signer.
