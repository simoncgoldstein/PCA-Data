# Batch 3 primary-source roster gaps

The following historical person-level rosters are confirmed by the PCA Digest but are intentionally **not normalized as named individuals** because the current retriever could not access the relevant pages of the very large official General Assembly PDFs. Counts and event-level actions are preserved in `sources/normalized/general-assembly/1973-2017-women-deacons-worship-chronology.json`.

## 1999, 27th General Assembly

Official minutes: `https://www.pcahistory.org/pca/ga/27th_pcaga_1999.pdf`

Requested primary-page recovery:

- p. 117-118, 27-31, MNA action: 61 commissioners recorded negative votes on the recommendation concerning MNA staff and women speaking/teaching.
- p. 170, 27-44, III.10: 144 commissioners recorded affirmative votes on the substitute to answer Western Carolina Presbytery Overture 16 affirmatively.
- pp. 211-212, 27-57: Protests 3 and 4 related to the women teaching/preaching controversy.

Official minutes: `https://www.pcahistory.org/pca/ga/28th_pcaga_2000.pdf`

Requested primary-page recovery:

- p. 101, 28-31, III.9: 16 commissioners registered affirmative votes on the defeated amendment to MNA Recommendation 9, and 20 commissioners registered negative votes on the adoption of Recommendation 9.

## 2002, 30th General Assembly

Official report family: `https://www.pcahistory.org/pca/studies/aiscwim.html`

The PCA Historical Center HTML confirms that 77 commissioners requested their negative vote be recorded after the Women in the Military minority substitute was defeated and the majority Recommendations 1-4 were adopted. The accessible HTML does not expose the 77-name roster. Do not create person-level vote records until the names are recovered from the official 30th General Assembly minutes.

## Modeling rule

A confirmed count is not a named roll call. These gaps remain explicit backlog items until the primary pages are available. Secondary reporting may be used to locate primary material but not to reconstruct missing official rosters by inference.
