- WUU describes itself publicly as a liberal religious congregation and maintains programs including Rainbow Ministry and religious-pluralism education.

- Robin's own current writing says she withdrew from the PCA in February 2023 and describes prior roles as a PCA local-church ministry leader and church-planter spouse/staff.
- Robin has published repeatedly on PCA race, authority, church-court, and denominational controversies. These writings are evidence of her public activity and self-description, but claims she makes about other persons or church courts require independent corroboration.
- Robin currently also appears in Asian American Christian Collaborative material and other public religious/social-justice settings.

## Why this belongs in the project

The analytically relevant trajectory is not a spouse-based inference. It is:

1. Robert Wootton held PCA teaching-elder and General Assembly roles.
2. He participated in a PCA racial-reconciliation publication network through *Hear Us, Emmanuel*.
3. Robin had ministry/staff involvement during the family's PCA period and publicly engaged PCA controversies.
4. The family subsequently left the PCA.
5. Robin now holds a formal religious-education position in a Unitarian Universalist congregation.

This is a useful **institutional trajectory** case. It should not be used to infer that every PCA participant in *Hear Us, Emmanuel* follows the same trajectory.
