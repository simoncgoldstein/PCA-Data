## Secondary sources

Secondary sources are used as roadmaps, corroboration, or interpretive context. They should not be the sole basis for a disputed formal-membership claim when a primary source is reasonably obtainable.

Current example:
- Presbyterian Polity, `Secret Caucuses & the PCA`.

## Archival policy

Where redistribution rights are unclear, the repository should store metadata, extraction notes, and the original URL rather than republishing the entire source file.
