# PCA withdrawal from the National Association of Evangelicals (2022)

## Primary source

Official Minutes of the 49th General Assembly (2022), volume 1:

https://pcahistory.org/pca/ga/49th_pcaga_2022_vol01.pdf

Key locations in the official minutes:

- Journal pp. 24–26: debate and final action on Overture 3.
- Journal pp. 27–32: Protest Regarding NAE Withdrawal and signer roster.
- Appendix N, pp. 412–419: Interchurch Relations Committee report.
- Addendum 1, pp. i–vii after p. 684: Committee of Commissioners on Interchurch Relations report.

## Verified event facts

- Overture 3 proposed that the PCA withdraw from the National Association of Evangelicals.
- The Interchurch Relations Permanent Committee recommended answering Overture 3 in the negative.
- The Committee of Commissioners offered a substitute recommending that Overture 3 be answered in the affirmative.
- TE Kenneth McHeard chaired the Committee of Commissioners presentation.
- TE L. Roy Taylor chaired the Permanent Committee and argued for continued membership.
- TE Carl Robbins argued for the Committee of Commissioners recommendation to withdraw.
- The final recorded vote adopted withdrawal, 1059–681.
- Dr. Walter Kim, then president of the NAE, provided fraternal greetings by video during the Assembly.
- TE Roy Taylor subsequently offered a formal protest against withdrawal. The protest stated, among other grounds, that withdrawal cast doubt on the PCA's practice of catholicity.
- The Minutes preserve a long named roster of commissioners who joined that protest. This roster should be normalized person-by-person as its own dataset.

## Walter Kim trajectory note

The same 49th GA minutes list Walter Kim among ministers dismissed to another denomination in 2021: Blue Ridge Presbytery, 2021-09-01, to the Conservative Congregational Christian Conference (CCCC). This is useful for trajectory/current-status modeling and should remain distinct from his role as NAE president.
