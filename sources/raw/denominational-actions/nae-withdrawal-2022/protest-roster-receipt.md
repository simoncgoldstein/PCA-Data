## Data rule

A signer receives only the literal edge:

`2022 NAE Withdrawal Protest — signer`

Do not infer that the signer agreed with every NAE statement or political position. The protest itself argued principally from church catholicity, the Westminster Confession's doctrine of the Church, and the IRC's case for continued affiliation.
