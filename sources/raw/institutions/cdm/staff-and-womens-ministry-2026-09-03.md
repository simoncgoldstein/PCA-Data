## Why this source family matters

CDM and Women's Ministry are formal PCA institutional structures. Their personnel should be mapped as institution roles with dated snapshots. This is especially relevant to the project's women-in-ministry research, but CDM employment or participation itself is **not** evidence that a person supports any disputed ecclesiastical practice.

The eventual graph should be able to distinguish:

- PCA denominational Women's Ministry role;
- local church staff role;
- seminary education;
- authored resource/publication;
- 2017 Women Serving in Ministry committee participation;
- AMR women's-office discussion;
- Save the PCA church-level assessments;
- any separately documented overture/protest/public-letter action.
