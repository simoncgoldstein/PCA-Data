## Denominational status

Covenant College identifies itself as the official college of the Presbyterian Church in America. Its board of trustees is elected/approved by the PCA General Assembly. The College states that trustees are PCA Teaching or Ruling Elders; its leadership structure also includes advisors drawn from alumni and representatives of the PCA's Women in the Church.

## Current president
