## Institutional status

Covenant states that it became the official seminary of the Presbyterian Church in America in 1982 and remains an official PCA agency. It also states that its Board of Trustees is approved annually by the PCA General Assembly and that all voting trustees are PCA Teaching Elders or Ruling Elders.

The Seminary describes itself as the largest supplier of pastors into the PCA.
