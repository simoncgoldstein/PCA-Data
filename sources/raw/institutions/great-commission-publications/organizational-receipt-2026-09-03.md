## Organizational relationship

Great Commission Publications identifies itself as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit **joint venture** of the Orthodox Presbyterian Church and the Presbyterian Church in America. It is governed by a representative Board of Trustees consisting of ministers and elders from both denominations.

Its historical account says the partnership was formalized on October 20, 1975, with the OPC and PCA each appointing six officers to the board, plus the relevant denominational Christian-education coordinators/general secretaries serving ex officio; GCP states that this board structure continues.

## Data-model rule

GCP is a PCA **partner/joint venture**, not one of the PCA's ordinary committees/agencies. Preserve the relationship type accordingly.
