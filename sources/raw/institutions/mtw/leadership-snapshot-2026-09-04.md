## Modeling rule

MTW employment, leadership, missionary service, and administrative roles are neutral institutional facts with **zero ideological weight**. Current-role claims require current official evidence. When a live but apparently stale conference page conflicts with a dated official biography recording a later departure or title change, preserve both source states and use the later dated event to determine current status.

Do not treat the Q3 2024 directory as a 2026 roster merely because the PDF remains publicly indexed.

## Current role changes and cautions

### Mark Bates

MTW's May and June 2026 articles repeatedly print `Senior Director of Missions Engagement`. That current title supersedes his `Senior Director of US Operations` title in the 2024 directory for current-role ingestion.

### David Stoddard / field operations

Stoddard's current missionary profile states that he serves both as International Director for Europe and Managing Director of Field Operations. This is current evidence of his expanded field-operations responsibility. It does **not**, by itself, establish an employment end date for Cartee Bales.

### Steve Robertson / Americas

The dated departure evidence is unusually clear: MTW states that Steve accepted a new call with Geneva Benefits Group in 2025. Therefore any MTW page still calling him International Director for the Americas is a stale role label and should be retained only as historical/source-drift evidence.

### John Tubbesing

The current Estate and Gift Planning page lists John Tubbesing simply under `Ambassadors Program`; it does not use the 2024 `CAO` title. Do not infer either continued CAO status or a precise departure date absent a clearer official source.
