## Why this source family is high priority

A major purpose of the project is to reconstruct institutional trajectories. Current and historical agency employment can connect people who otherwise appear only in isolated public actions.

Examples of analytically useful paths:

- Seminary → RUF → local pastorate → MNA leadership.
- Local pastorate → MTW → General Assembly committee.
- CDM / Women's Ministry → study committee / publication / AMR discussion.
- RUF cross-cultural staff → racial-reconciliation initiatives → public statements.
- MNA subministry → public controversy → later denominational leadership.

Agency employment itself is not ideological evidence and normally receives zero Network Involvement Index weight.

## Required current roster snapshots

### Administrative Committee

Capture:
- Stated Clerk and executive leadership;
- senior staff;
- byFaith leadership/editors where public;
- Historical Center leadership where organizationally housed or formally connected;
- General Assembly operations staff where analytically relevant.

### Committee on Discipleship Ministries

Capture:
- Coordinator / executive leadership;
- Women's Ministry leadership;
- Men's Ministry leadership;
- Children's Ministry leadership;
- other standing ministry teams with named public staff.

### Covenant College

Capture:
- president and cabinet;
- trustees if public and reasonably extractable;
- current faculty only where a person intersects the PCA research universe or where a complete structured faculty import is feasible.

Covenant College is a denominational institution, but seminary/faculty analysis receives higher initial priority than broad undergraduate faculty analysis.

### Covenant Theological Seminary

Highest priority institutional roster beyond the church/GA datasets.

Capture:
- president/chancellor;
- all current faculty;
- emeritus faculty;
- adjunct/visiting faculty when clearly listed;
- academic deans;
- trustees if public;
- key centers/institutes;
- faculty biographies, including education and former ministry roles.

### PCA Foundation

Capture executive staff, board/trustees where public, and prior denominational roles stated in bios.

### Mission to North America

Capture:
- Coordinator and senior leadership;
- African American Ministries;
- Refugee & Immigrant Ministry;
- Church Planting;
- Church Vitality / revitalization programs;
- Chaplain Ministries;
- Disaster Response;
- Metanoia Prison Ministries if institutionally within MNA at the snapshot date;
- Ministry to State where organizationally applicable;
- other named staff/subministries that recur in the person graph.

Historical roster snapshots matter because MNA personnel have appeared repeatedly in racial-reconciliation, immigration, church-planting, minority-leadership, and public-statement datasets.

### Mission to the World

Capture:
- Coordinator / executive leadership;
- senior leadership team;
- regional directors;
- major strategy/team directors;
- current missionaries only when they already appear in another high-value dataset or when an institutional roster can be imported responsibly.

The complete global missionary population may be too large and privacy-sensitive for indiscriminate ingestion. Prioritize public leadership and named public-facing staff.

### Reformed University Fellowship

Capture:
- Coordinator / national leadership;
- assistant coordinators;
- cross-cultural advancement leadership;
- regional staff;
- current campus ministers;
- campus and presbytery where stated;
- previous RUF roles in bios.

RUF is especially valuable because it creates a long-term ministry pipeline from seminary into local PCA ministry and denominational leadership.

### PCA Retirement & Benefits

Capture executive leadership and board where public. Preserve historical naming changes separately if the organization rebrands or restructures.

### Ridge Haven

Capture executive leadership, board where public, and major program leadership.

## Snapshot policy

For every live roster:

- record `snapshot_date`;
- preserve exact title as printed;
- preserve source URL;
- preserve individual bio URL;
- preserve archived URL when available;
- do not overwrite a prior snapshot when staff change.

The future graph should be able to answer both `where is this person now?` and `who occupied this institutional role in 2017?`

## Historical-source expansion

After current rosters are captured, backfill leadership through:

1. General Assembly annual agency reports;
2. archived organization staff pages;
3. byFaith announcements of appointments/departures;
4. institutional annual reports;
5. official biographies.

General Assembly minutes are particularly valuable because committee/agency reports often preserve board members, staff changes, and formal appointments in a consistent annual series.
