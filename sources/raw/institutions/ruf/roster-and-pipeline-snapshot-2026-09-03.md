## Why RUF is a high-priority graph institution

RUF explicitly describes itself as preparing college students for long-term service in the church and has functioned as a major PCA ministry pipeline since 1973. The official history records:

- first internship in 1976;
- formal PCA ministry principles in 1979;
- Internship program in 1980;
- recurring staff training beginning at RTS Jackson in 1981;
- expansion to major universities nationwide;
- first Campus Staff role created in 2015 to provide women a formal place serving college students through RUF;
- RUF International and RUF Global expansion.
