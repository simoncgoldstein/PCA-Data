# Immigration, refugees, and the PCA

This dossier tracks formal PCA ministries, denominational statements/actions, and external evangelical coalitions connected to immigration/refugee questions. It should remain distinct from the National Partnership unless an NP source itself establishes a connection.

## Mission to North America: Refugee and Immigrant Ministry

### Formation

Official 43rd GA Minutes (2015):
https://www.pcahistory.org/pca/ga/43rd_pcaga_2015.pdf

The MNA report states that during 2014, building on initiatives begun by World Relief, MNA added Refugee and Immigrant Ministry as a new Outreach Ministry with Pat Hatch as director. The report describes a goal of helping PCA congregations develop ministry among immigrant neighbors.

### Historical director: Pat Hatch

MNA biography:
https://resources.pcamna.org/resource/refugee-and-immigrant-ministry_pat-hatch-bio/

Hatch states that she took the MNA Refugee and Immigrant Ministry Director role in July 2014 after decades of immigrant/refugee work, including refugee resettlement and state refugee-office programs.

MNA ministry resource:
https://resources.pcamna.org/resource/refugee-and-immigrant-ministry/

The resource presents immigration as a Great Commission and mercy-ministry opportunity and urges PCA churches to see immigrants regardless of country of origin or immigration status primarily as image-bearers and potential recipients of gospel ministry.

### Current director: Angela Pacey

MNA current staff:
https://www.pcamna.org/about

MNA 2025 ministries brochure:
https://pcamna.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/MNA-Ministries-Brochure-2025.pdf

These identify Angela Pacey as Refugee & Immigrant Ministry Director. Current team material also identifies Osman Jama as Refugee Church Engagement Specialist and Leslie Johnson as Associate Director.

MNA announced Pacey as director effective 2024-11-01, succeeding Pat Hatch.

### 2025 immigration-resource controversy

byFaith / MNA statement, 2025-02-12:
https://byfaithonline.com/statement-from-mission-to-north-america/

In a statement issued by MNA Coordinator Irwyn Ince, MNA acknowledged that its Refugee and Immigrant Ministry webpage had linked to external material that included advice to undocumented persons on avoiding detention by authorities. Ince said MNA leadership had erroneously allowed the content to be posted. This event should be modeled as a specific 2025 MNA content/oversight incident, not as a generalized claim about all MNA immigration work.

### Afghan and Ukrainian refugee work

MNA resources document church assistance/resettlement resources concerning Afghan arrivals after the 2021 evacuation and Ukrainians after Russia's 2022 invasion. These should be represented as ministry-program activity rather than political-policy advocacy unless a source explicitly contains policy advocacy.

## Evangelical Immigration Table / NAE

Evangelical Immigration Table:
https://evangelicalimmigrationtable.com/about/

The EIT describes itself as advocating for a bipartisan immigration solution that includes secure borders, rule of law, family unity, fairness to taxpayers, and a path toward legal status/citizenship for qualifying immigrants.

The current national-principals roster identifies **Walter Kim, President of the National Association of Evangelicals**, among the principals of EIT leadership organizations.

This creates a current trajectory edge:

Walter Kim → NAE (President) → Evangelical Immigration Table (principal through NAE)

It does not by itself establish that the PCA as a denomination endorses EIT, especially because Kim had been dismissed from Blue Ridge Presbytery to the Conservative Congregational Christian Conference in 2021 and the PCA withdrew from NAE in 2022.

## World Relief relationship

The PCA's 2015 MNA report says Refugee and Immigrant Ministry built on initiatives begun by World Relief. World Relief is historically connected to the NAE and appears in refugee-resettlement resources used by PCA ministries. Model this as institutional collaboration/source use, not denominational control.

## A Faithful PCA

The June 2, 2021 `Looking Forward – Together` letter explicitly celebrates `refugee care` among developments it considers a healthy biblical consciousness in the PCA. This is a direct coalition-position marker and should be linked to that public-letter event once its clean signer roster is recovered.

## NP archive status

An exact-term scan of the combined 2013–2021 National Partnership archive on 2026-09-03 found no literal matches for `immigrant`, `migrant`, `refugee`, or `refugees`. This does not establish absence. Semantic searching and alternate terminology remain possible, but no NP immigration position should be asserted from the archive without direct evidence.
