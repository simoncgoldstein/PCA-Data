# Overtures 23 and 37 authored position sources

Captured: 2026-09-04

## Purpose

Preserve direct authored person-level evidence from the December 16, 2021 byFaith paired essays on PCA Overtures 23 and 37. These sources are modeled as the authors' stated positions and rationales, not as positions of byFaith, their presbyteries, GRN, the Standing Judicial Commission, or any other organization with which they were associated.

## Source 1

- Author: Jon D. Payne
- Title: `Recommending Overtures 23 and 37`
- Published: 2021-12-16
- URL: `https://byfaithonline.com/recommending-overtures-23-and-37/`
- Evidence class: direct authored position

Bounded findings:

- Payne explicitly advocates ratification of both Overture 23 and Overture 37.
- On Overture 23, he argues that the amendment gives constitutional clarity concerning officer qualifications, identity language, fallen desires, progressive sanctification, and pursuit of holiness.
- On Overture 37, he argues that the amendment appropriately raises and clarifies standards for officer examinations and gives sessions and presbyteries useful guidance.
- He presents the contemporary sexual revolution and what he describes as Revoice-related pressures as part of his rationale for adopting the amendments.

## Source 2

- Author: David Coffin
- Title: `Against Overtures 23 and 37`
- Published: 2021-12-16
- URL: `https://byfaithonline.com/against-overtures-23-and-37/`
- Evidence class: direct authored position

Bounded findings:

- Coffin explicitly argues against ratification of both Overture 23 and Overture 37.
- His central polity argument is that the BCO should state fundamental scriptural principles and stable procedures rather than attempt to settle every controversy.
- He argues that the proposed amendments were not clearly necessary and lacked sufficiently mature consideration.
- He argues that Overture 23 contains difficult or potentially confusing identity-language constructions.
- He argues that Overture 37 introduces unclear examination language, including the phrases concerning potentially notorious concerns and reputation or self-profession according to remaining sinfulness.
- He concludes that the existing BCO examination standards are sufficient unless a clearer necessity for amendment is shown.

- Their organizational roles and affiliations do not cause these positions to propagate to other people or institutions.
- Agreement or disagreement with an overture does not, by itself, establish a person's complete theology of sexuality, sanctification, identity language, Revoice, or church polity.
