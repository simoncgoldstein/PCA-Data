# Racial reconciliation / ethnic diversity in the PCA

This dossier tracks the formal denominational sequence and the overlapping voluntary-publication/network activity. It is designed to distinguish General Assembly actions from private-network organizing and from later public coalitions.

## Official source index

PCA Historical Center topical archive:
https://www.pcahistory.org/topical/race/index.html

The archive provides a useful documentary chronology including PCA actions in 1973, 2002, 2004, the 2015 Personal Resolution on Civil Rights Remembrance, 2016 overtures, and the 2018 Ad Interim Committee report.

## 2015: Personal Resolution on Civil Rights Remembrance

Official 43rd GA actions:
https://www.pcahistory.org/pca/ga/actions/43rdGA_2015_Actions.pdf

The official summary states that TE Sean Lucas and TE Ligon Duncan introduced a Personal Resolution calling for the Assembly, around the fiftieth anniversary of major Civil Rights-era events, to express repentance for failures by churches and Christians to stand in solidarity with African-American brothers and sisters and to pursue racial reconciliation adequately. The Overtures Committee spent approximately nine hours on the matter and unanimously recommended referral to the 2016 Assembly for further consideration.

### National Partnership overlap

The combined NP email archive contains Lucas's resolution and internal organizing around it. Lucas described the proposed language as recognizing covenantal and generational involvement/complicity with racial injustice and said it had been circulated among key Black PCA pastors and through RAAN for feedback. James Kessler urged NP members to be present for the two-thirds vote needed for referral to the Overtures Committee.

This is a strong network-action edge because it involves an explicitly identified NP member, an overture/resolution text, internal circulation, and floor-presence organizing.

## 2016: forty-three racial-reconciliation overtures and Overture 43

Official 44th GA actions:
https://www.pcahistory.org/pca/ga/actions/44thGA_2016_Actions.pdf

The official summary says **43 overtures** dealing with racial reconciliation came from presbyteries in 2016. Overture 4 from Missouri served as a model for many. The Overtures Committee used Overture 43 from Potomac as a framework, incorporating elements from Missouri, Northwest Georgia, and Mississippi Valley. The amended Overture 43 was adopted; the remaining racial-reconciliation overtures were answered by reference to it.

The General Assembly also approved Overture 45 from Potomac, forming an Ad Interim Committee on Racial Reconciliation and Ethnic Diversity.

### NP overlap

NP correspondence says Lucas and Duncan co-authored the resolution lineage and explicitly encouraged members to move a preferred covenantal-responsibility form or affirmations through their presbyteries. Kessler later described the adopted language as an initial act of recognition and repentance.

## 2016 PCA Unity Fund

The Assembly established a PCA Unity Fund administered through MNA to help raise up future generations of African-American and other ethnic-minority Teaching and Ruling Elders. This should be a separate institution/program node, with staff, committee leadership, grants, and subsequent denominational reports normalized independently.

## Later coalition continuity

### A Faithful PCA / Looking Forward – Together (2021)

The letter explicitly celebrates racial reconciliation, refugee care, the Unity Fund's minority-leadership work, and increased attention to the role of women and other mercy/ministry issues.

### A Call to Prayer & Lament (2026)

The statement explicitly addresses perceived lack of progress on racial reconciliation, treatment of minority leaders, women, LGBTQ individuals, and immigrants. Organizers and initial signers must be separated from the wider commitment form, whose later submissions experienced verification/spam problems.
