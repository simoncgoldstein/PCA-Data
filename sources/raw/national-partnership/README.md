# National Partnership Raw Sources

This folder will preserve the National Partnership email archive and related first-party/public records used to identify membership, additions, committee placement, organizing activity, candidate support, and presbytery coverage.

## Evidence classes to preserve separately

- Explicit member / addition
- Founder / organizer
- Committee placement explicitly described as a Partnership member/man
- Recruitment or presbytery coordination
- Candidate recommended or supported by the Partnership
- Event participant or adjacent figure without demonstrated membership

Do not infer membership from praise, correspondence, attendance, or candidate support alone.

## Planned archive

- Annual email files, 2013–2021, preferably original PDFs or the cleanest public mirrors available
- Combined archive where useful for search
- Provenance and checksums in `../../manifests/`
- Normalized member and activity tables in `../../extracts/national-partnership/`
