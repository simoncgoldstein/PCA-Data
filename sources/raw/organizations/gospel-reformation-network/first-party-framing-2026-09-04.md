# Gospel Reformation Network first-party framing receipt

Captured: 2026-09-04

Primary source: https://gospelreformation.net/the-gospel-reformation-network-a-new-vision-for-continued-faithfulness-in-the-pca/

## Source scope

This source is a first-party Gospel Reformation Network statement describing the network's history, original sanctification focus, expanded vision, and concerns for the future of the PCA.

## Bounded claims preserved

1. GRN says it began in 2012 among PCA pastors to promote a proper articulation of and call to sanctification, including the third use of the law, biblical imperatives, pastoral piety, spiritual disciplines, and personal holiness.
2. GRN says it later widened its scope from sanctification alone to cultivating healthy Reformed churches and maintaining the PCA's long-term biblical and confessional integrity.
3. GRN explicitly identifies biblical fidelity and confessional integrity as core distinctives, including Scripture's inspiration, inerrancy, authority, sufficiency, and efficacy together with adherence to the Westminster Standards.
4. GRN says certain progressive trajectories in the PCA resemble patterns seen historically in liberal mainline churches and identifies concerns including social-justice/cultural-transformational substitution for biblical mission and discipleship, lack of confessional integrity, deficient Reformed worship and means of grace, insufficient pastoral piety, and confusion over sexuality and gender roles.
