Jude 3 & the PCA describes itself as an independent voice consisting of PCA members. It says it has operated since 2021.

The organization says it works for the glory of Christ through pastoral and confessional accountability within the PCA.

It says its earlier work primarily aggregated news articles, blogs, podcasts, and other media while maintaining an online archive of resources.

### Current publishing priority

The About page says Jude 3 & the PCA is shifting from primarily highlighting material published by others toward publishing and distributing more of its own content.

### Editorial aim

It invites submissions from PCA officers and members aimed at encouraging confessional fidelity, integrity, and vitality.

### Westminster Standard relationship

The About page says the organization continues to sponsor The Westminster Standard Podcast as part of its publishing activity.
