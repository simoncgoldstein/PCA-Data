# MORE in the PCA — first-party framing receipt

Captured: 2026-09-04

Primary source:
- https://jude3pca.org/more/

The page identifies MORE in the PCA as a grassroots movement of PCA Ruling Elders. It says the movement seeks to encourage Ruling Elders who have not been active in Presbytery and General Assembly to begin attending and participating.

The page grounds this in BCO 8-3, emphasizing Ruling Elders' obligation to exercise government and discipline and to take oversight of the Church generally when called thereunto.

The page argues that low Ruling Elder participation at General Assembly weakens the PCA's intended plurality of elders, noting a 4:1 Teaching Elder to Ruling Elder ratio at the 2017 General Assembly and describing that imbalance as a problem for confessional operation.

Its stated practical goal is to increase Ruling Elder participation in church courts, including providing financial support for Ruling Elders who otherwise could not attend General Assembly.

Modeling boundary:
- These are MORE's own stated organizational purposes and diagnoses as presented on its first-party page.
- The page is hosted on Jude 3 & the PCA, but MORE is modeled as a distinct grassroots movement rather than as identical to Jude 3.
- The claim about 2017 General Assembly confessional operation is preserved as MORE's claim, not independently adjudicated by this project.
- No individual Ruling Elder automatically inherits every organizational position merely through participation or support.
