## Bounded source-derived observations

### Organizational purpose

The About page states that Save the PCA was started by Michael Foster and other named and anonymous churchmen. It describes its goal as stopping the PCA from embracing left-wing practices that it judges not to be rooted in Scripture, specifically naming female ordination as an example of the kind of development it seeks to prevent. It also describes its method as organizing PCA members and conducting an awareness campaign.

### Functional Female Officer definition

The follow-up article defines a functional female officer, for purposes of the report, as a named female role that performs one or more functions Save the PCA regards as typically reserved to elders and/or deacons and that undergoes some form of commissioning or ordination-like process.

The article explicitly excludes roles described merely as assistants to the diaconate from the functional-female-deacon classification because such assistance is permitted by the BCO. It also says women's ministry coordinators were explicitly omitted from the functional-female-elder dataset.

### Public worship and governance concern

The report states that its concern includes women performing officer-like functions in public worship and church governance. It lists examples such as calls to worship, Scripture reading, pastoral prayers, confessions, exhortations, distribution of the Lord's Supper elements alongside elders, shepherding boards, and elder-advisor roles.

The report argues that examination, officer-style training, election or nomination, commissioning, public vows, and participation in officer-like functions can make a formally non-ordained role functionally analogous to church office.

### Trajectory claim

The report argues that normalizing these practices creates a trajectory toward formal female eldership in the PCA. This is Save the PCA's stated analysis and should not be represented as an independently established causal fact.

### Method and action framing

The report says its evidence comes from publicly available church websites, livestreams, and church documents. Its calls to action emphasize raising awareness and improving/expanding the dataset through verifiable evidence.

The follow-up article explicitly says that not every row in the supporting data is itself classified as a functional female officer. The church-level determination is separate from the raw person/role observations.

## Modeling guardrails

- These statements document Save the PCA's own organizational framing and methodology.
- They do not independently establish that every church or person flagged by Save the PCA violates Scripture or PCA polity.
- A source-attributed church classification must not automatically become a personal theological position for every pastor, staff member, officer, or congregant at that church.
- Raw role observations and Save the PCA's interpretive classifications remain distinct evidence layers.
