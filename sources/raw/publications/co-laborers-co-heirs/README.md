## Affiliation notes visible in the screenshots

The screenshots also contain current or last verified PCA church/presbytery notes. These are useful trajectory leads, but they should be separately verified against church/institution sources before becoming current-role facts in the main person graph.

Examples visible in the images include:

- Sean Michael Lucas — Independent Presbyterian Church; Covenant Presbytery; senior pastor / RTS church-history professor; AMR team member.
- Scott Sauls — Christ Presbyterian Church (Nashville), Nashville Presbytery as last PCA affiliation; later leads Healthy Leaders and the Sycamore Community.
- Doug Serven — Grace Reformed Presbyterian Church, Chesapeake Presbytery; editor of the volume.
- Brittany Smith — University Presbyterian Church (Las Cruces), Rio Grande Presbytery; Covenant Seminary graduate; formerly served with RUF.
- John Haralson / Debbie Tacke — Grace Church Seattle as last PCA affiliation; later denominational movement noted.
- Sarah Viggiano Wright — Trinity Grace Church, South Texas; screenshot notes Covenant MDiv and MAC and RUF ministry partnership.
- Suzanne R. Bates — New City Fellowship (University City), Missouri; screenshot notes Covenant Seminary faculty/counseling role.

These notes are **lead-level biographical evidence from the source-audit screenshots**, not substitutes for current official bios.

## Research use

This record belongs in the women-in-ministry/publication layer. Contribution to the book should not by itself be encoded as egalitarianism or as support for female ordination. The graph should record the factual publication role, then separately connect any contributor to documented overtures, study committees, organizational roles, public statements, church practices, denominational moves, or institutional appointments where independently established.
