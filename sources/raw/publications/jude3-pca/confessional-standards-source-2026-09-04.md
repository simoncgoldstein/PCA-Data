Captured: 2026-09-04

## Source

Title: `PCA Confessional Standards`

URL: https://jude3pca.org/pca-confessional-standards/

Attribution status: site-level first-party page.

## Explicit Jude 3 statements used

The page explicitly says Jude 3 & the PCA believes:

- periodic review and renewal of commitment to the Westminster Standards is healthy;
- the Westminster Standards are subordinate to Scripture but are a good and necessary summary of Bible doctrine;
- time-bound critical methods, Critical Theory, pervasive interpretive pluralism, and other culturally approved critical tools have no authority when considering Bible doctrine;
- dismissing the Westminster divines because of their historical period is rejected as chronological snobbery;
- the Westminster Standards are presented as the product of sustained Scriptural study and Reformed collaboration.

## Boundary

Immediately after these Jude 3-specific statements, the page says that the **Firm Foundation Partnership** endorses strict adherence to the Westminster Standards qualified only by Scripture. That FFP statement is not normalized here as a Jude 3 organizational position.
